# Learning map: shaders for creative coding

## Interest

- Writing and editing GLSL shaders for creative coding.

## Methodology

- Find a way to make examples quickly.
- Find a way to get inspiration, to come up with visual ideas that translate properly into code.

## Software

- [LYGIA](https://lygia.xyz/) - To prototype shaders quickly with ready-made functions.
- [Lens Studio](https://ar.snap.com/lens-studio) - To learn quickly with small results: Snapchat filters.
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) - To learn the basics of geometry.
- [three.js](https://threejs.org/) - To see what is easy or hard in 3D, and what a more interesting piece looks like.
- [TouchDesigner](https://derivative.ca/) - To see how a nodal workflow works, and to try my own shaders in its [GLSL TOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/GLSL_TOP).
- Still looking for: a tool to publish work in small pieces and get feedback quickly.

## Resources

- [The Book of Shaders](https://thebookofshaders.com/) - To learn fragment shaders step by step.
- [Inigo Quilez, articles](https://iquilezles.org/articles/) - To understand the mathematics behind shader techniques.
- [The Coding Train](https://thecodingtrain.com/) - Where I started with creative coding.
- [Introduction to Linear Algebra](https://math.mit.edu/~gs/linearalgebra/) - For the vectors and matrices behind shaders. Gilbert Strang, 6th edition, 2023.
- [MIT 18.06SC Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/) - The same author's lectures, to follow along with the book.

## Communities

- [CC Fest](https://ccfest.rocks/) - To get inspired and learn from other creative coders.
- Still looking for: a community to share work and get real feedback from peers.

## Challenges

- I tried The Coding Train, but it was hard to know what else is out there.
- The Book of Shaders is not complete: the chapters after Fractional Brownian Motion (fractals, image processing, simulation, 3D graphics) are not written yet.
- I do not know yet if I can write or edit existing shaders in TouchDesigner, for a workflow that I can extend.
